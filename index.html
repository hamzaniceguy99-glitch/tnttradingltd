Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do you cut Afro and curly hair?

Yes, and we cut it dry. Curly and Afro hair behaves differently wet and dry, so cutting it wet means guessing at the finished shape — which is why so many cuts look wrong once they dry.

Can you do the cut in this photo?

We will tell you honestly. Hair texture, density, hairline and growth pattern decide a great deal, and the person in the photo may simply have different hair to yours.

I have coloured my hair for years. Do I need a patch test?

Yes. Sensitisation can develop after years without any problem, which is exactly why the test is repeated. If your last one is out of date we will decline the colour.

Is a hot towel shave safe for me?

Usually, but tell us about skin conditions, cold sores, recent facial treatments or blood thinners first. Sometimes the right answer is to wait, and we will say so.

Are your tools cleaned between clients?

Yes, cleaned and disinfected every time, and razor blades are single use. It is worth asking this wherever you go.

Do I need an appointment?

Not necessarily — walk in and we will fit you in when a chair is free. At weekends an appointment is worth having, because the wait can be long.

Due a cut?

Book a chair, or walk in and we will fit you in when one is free.
