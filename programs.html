Program 02 · Cut dry, shaped to growth

Textured and Afro hair

Curly and Afro hair behaves differently wet and dry, so cutting it wet means guessing at the finished shape. We cut dry, work with the growth pattern and shrinkage rather than against them, and we will not sell you a shape that needs daily work to hold.

  • 45 to 60 minutes
  • All ages
  • Per cut
  • Come with hair dry

Start this program

Program 03 · Single-use blades, always

Beards and hot towel shaves

A beard line cut too high looks sharp today and wrong in a fortnight, so we set it where growing out stays tidy. Blades are single use, every time. Tell us about skin conditions, cold sores, recent facial treatments or blood thinners first — sometimes the answer is not today.

  • 20 to 45 minutes
  • Adults
  • Per service
  • Disclose conditions

Program 04 · No patch test, no colour

Colour

The patch test is not a formality. Reactions to colour can be severe and can develop in people who have had it done for years, so we will decline a service where the test is out of date — which occasionally annoys people and is not negotiable.

  • 1 to 3 hours
  • Adults
  • Per service
  • Valid patch test
